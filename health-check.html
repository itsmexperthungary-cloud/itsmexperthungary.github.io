ITSM Health Check

A clear picture of your service management in five days.

A short, fixed-⁠scope assessment for IT organisations that want to know where they stand and where to start, before committing to a larger change.

Book a 20-⁠minute intro call

Who it is for

You are starting or restarting ITSM

You need an honest baseline before you design processes or choose a tool.

Processes exist, but results are uneven

Adoption varies between teams, and nobody is quite sure why.

Something big is coming

A new leader, a reorganisation or an audit, and you need a clear view fast.

How it works

Three steps, five working days

  1. Day 1

    Kick-⁠off and scope

    We agree on your goals, the practices in scope and who I will talk to.

  2. Days 2–4

    Interviews and review

    6–8 interviews with IT leaders, service owners and resolver teams, plus a look at your process documents, tool setup and key metrics.

  3. Day 5

    Readout

    A half-⁠day session with your leadership team to walk through the findings and agree on what comes first.

What you get

  • A maturity snapshotWhere each practice in scope stands today, based on ITIL 4.
  • A stakeholder viewWhere people agree, where they do not, and what is holding adoption back.
  • Three prioritiesConcrete next steps you can start on the following week, with or without me.
  • A short written reportClear enough to share with your management and teams.
