When you contact me

If you email me, I process your name, email address and the content of your message to reply to you and, where relevant, to prepare a possible cooperation. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)⁠(b)) and my legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)⁠(f)). I keep this correspondence for up to one year after our last contact, unless it becomes part of a contract, in which case the retention periods required by law apply.

When you book a call

Intro calls are booked on a Google Calendar booking page, a service of Google. When you book, I receive your name, your email address and, if you add one, a short note about what you would like to discuss. Google Calendar also creates a Google Meet link for the call. I use these details only to hold the call and to follow up on it. The legal basis is the same as for emails (GDPR Article 6(1)⁠(b) and (f)). I delete the booking within one year after the call, unless it leads to a contract. For the booking page itself, see the Google Privacy Policy.
